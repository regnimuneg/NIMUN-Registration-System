🎯 JNIMUN'25 Test QR Codes

Use these QR codes to test the scanner functionality in your JNIMUN system.

📋 How to test:

  1. Open your JNIMUN system dashboard
  2. Go to the "Scan QR Code" tab
  3. Click "Activate Scanner" or "Start Scanner"
  4. Point your camera at any QR code below
  5. The system should automatically detect and process the participant

✅ Test Results:

Expected behavior:

  • Camera should start and show video feed
  • QR code should be detected automatically (no clicking needed)
  • Participant information should load instantly
  • You should see success messages and scan count increase
  • Attendance/tracking tabs should become available

⚠️ Troubleshooting:

  • Camera not working: Check browser permissions, use HTTPS
  • QR not detected: Ensure good lighting, hold steady, try different distance
  • Permission denied: Click camera icon in browser address bar, allow access
  • No camera found: Use "Upload Image" or "Manual Input" alternatives
